Privacy Policy — Smriti

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Smriti ("we", "our", "the service") is a WhatsApp-based platform that helps grandparents preserve family memories by sending voice notes and photos, which are transcribed, restored, and used to generate memoir content. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Smriti via WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Voice notes you send, which are transcribed into text.
  • Photos you send, which are processed for restoration (colorization, scratch removal, and optional upscaling).
  • Your WhatsApp phone number (WhatsApp ID), used to identify you and send responses.
  • Message metadata such as timestamps, needed to route and process your requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you send strictly to provide the Smriti service to you, including:

  • Transcribing voice notes into text memoirs.
  • Restoring and colorizing photos you send us.
  • Generating voice responses using voice cloning technology, where you have opted in.
  • Sending processed results (transcriptions, restored photos, memoir content) back to you via WhatsApp.

We do not use your voice notes, photos, or messages for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. How We Process Your Data

Your voice notes and photos are sent to backend processing services (for transcription, photo restoration, and voice cloning) that we operate. These services process your content to generate the memoir output you requested. Processed data may be temporarily stored to generate your requested output and for debugging purposes, and is not shared outside the services required to fulfill your request.

4. Data Retention

We retain your voice notes, photos, and generated content only as long as necessary to provide the service to you and to allow you to retrieve your memoir content. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see "Your Rights" below).

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is only shared with the backend processing services required to fulfill your requests (e.g. transcription and photo restoration services), which operate under the same data protection commitments as this policy.

6. Your Rights

You may, at any time:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your voice notes, photos, and generated memoir content.
  • Stop using the service at any time by simply no longer messaging the Smriti WhatsApp number.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including secure transmission of data between WhatsApp, our services, and processing backends. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Smriti is designed for use by adults, particularly grandparents preserving family memories. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
