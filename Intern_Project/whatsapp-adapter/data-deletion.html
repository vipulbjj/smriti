Data Deletion Instructions — Smriti

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Smriti processes voice notes, photos, and messages you send via WhatsApp in order to generate transcriptions, restored photos, and memoir content. You can request deletion of your data at any time using the instructions below.

How to Request Deletion

To request that we delete your data, choose one of the following options:

  1. Message us directly: Send the message "DELETE MY DATA" to the Smriti WhatsApp number. We will process your request and confirm once your data has been removed from our systems.
  2. Email us: Send a deletion request to vbajaj56@gmail.com from the email or with the WhatsApp number associated with your account, and we will process the request within a reasonable timeframe.

What Gets Deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, we will remove:

  • Voice notes and photos you have sent to the service.
  • Transcriptions, restored images, and generated memoir content associated with your WhatsApp number.
  • Your WhatsApp ID and any associated message metadata stored in our systems.

Timeframe

We aim to complete data deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe of receiving a verified request. You will receive a confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Third-Party Processing Services

Your content may have been processed by backend services we operate for transcription, photo restoration, and voice cloning. Deletion requests will also be applied to these processing services where your content was temporarily stored.
